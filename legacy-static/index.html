How we make the work

Research, narrative, form, technology, fabrication.

Chromosome Designs works across the complete journey: interpreting the brief, mapping the audience, designing the spatial script, producing exhibits, developing interactive media, and resolving on-site details.

Studio philosophy
01Research and site reading

02Concept and story architecture

03Design and prototyping

Models, materials, software flows, AR/VR tests, exhibit drawings, and fabrication logic.

04Production and installation

Vendor coordination, site supervision, quality control, lighting, launch, and handover.
